# Changelog

## [1.1.0]

- 17 wildcard cards for what stands out in your year, with up to 3 per year: comebacks after a pause or after years, a book that waited for you, a book read in a single day, slow burns, rereads, series runs, books read at once, books you almost finished, your shortest and longest books, your most marked book, the first and last books of the year, your reading ritual, nights past midnight, a day to remember and each book's pace.
- Series are also recognized from the title, since most files have no series metadata.
- Cards drop site marks, website addresses, edition tags, "Microsoft Word" prefixes and file extensions from titles.
- Generic labels such as "Unknown" and "Windows User", websites and very short names no longer count as authors, and several authors in one field are separated.
- Very long titles no longer leave the screen, cut sentences short or overlap the drawing on generated covers.
- The secondary trait is now a small label right below the profile card: "With traits of...".

## [1.0.0]

First public release.

- Up to 18 illustrated cards with your year of reading, in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
- Shelf, year map, 24-hour clock, timeline of finished books, devoured book, top author and comparison with the previous year.
- Reading profile with eight illustrations, chosen by your strongest trait, with the next one as a secondary touch.
- Book covers, with a generated cover for books that have none.
- Card images saved to KOReader's screenshots folder.
- Everything stays on the device, with no internet.
