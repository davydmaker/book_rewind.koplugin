# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

First public release.

- Up to 18 illustrated cards with your year of reading, in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
- Shelf, year map, 24-hour clock, timeline of finished books, devoured book, top author and comparison with the previous year.
- Reading profile with eight illustrations, chosen by your strongest trait, with the next one as a secondary touch.
- Book covers, with a generated cover for books that have none.
- Card images saved to KOReader's screenshots folder.
- Everything stays on the device, with no internet.
